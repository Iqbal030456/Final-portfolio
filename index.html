Smartfarm

Mini greenhouse

A smart farm built like a miniature greenhouse. It keeps watch on the plants so conditions can be checked and corrected without opening it up.

  1. SenseSensors take regular readings of the growing conditions inside the greenhouse.
  2. DecideA microcontroller compares each reading with the range the plants need.
  3. ActWhen a reading drifts out of range, the controller switches the matching output on or off, for example a fan or a pump.
  4. ShowThe latest readings are displayed so the plants can be monitored at a glance.

Automation

Automatic cat feeder

A feeder that uses AI to recognise a cat coming towards it, so food is only served when a cat is actually there.

  1. WatchA camera keeps an eye on the area in front of the feeder.
  2. DetectAn AI model checks what the camera sees and recognises an incoming cat.
  3. FeedOnce a cat is detected, the feeder is triggered to serve food.
